## About me

I am an Associate Professor of Linguistics in the Department of English Language, College of Language Sciences, King Saud University. My research investigates how prosody, intonation and acoustic cues encode meaning in Arabic dialects, and how linguistic theory and speech data can inform computational models. My work also spans Arabic natural language processing, dialect identification, speech annotation, corpus development and AI-assisted research tools.

I earned my PhD in Linguistics (2014) and MA in Linguistics with Distinction (2010) from the University of Essex, UK, and my BA in English Language with teacher preparation (2006) from Taif University, Saudi Arabia.

## Research interests

- **Phonetics and prosody:** focus marking, intonation, acoustic analysis, speech production and perception.
- **Arabic dialectology:** Saudi and other Arabic varieties, dialect variation and speech corpora.
- **Computational linguistics and NLP:** Arabic text processing, morphological and dependency annotation, dialect classification.
- **Speech technology:** acoustic feature extraction, annotation pipelines, computational modeling and reproducible experiments.
- **Research software and AI:** Python- and R-based workflows, machine learning and digital research infrastructure.
